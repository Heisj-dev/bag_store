Bags & Beyond

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Bags & Beyond sells bags in Uganda. This page explains what personal information we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, who sees it and what your choices are. We handle your information in line with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a password. We keep only a scrambled version of the password, never the password itself. If you continue with Google, Google gives us your name and email address. We never see your Google password.
  • Your orders: your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, city or area, delivery notes and the bags you ordered.
  • Cookies: we use only the cookies the website needs to work: one that keeps you signed in and remembers your cart, and one that protects our forms from forgery. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Fonts: our pages load the Archivo font from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch it.

How we use it

  • To take, confirm, deliver and support your order, including a phone or WhatsApp call to confirm your drop-off location.
  • To email you about your order (received, confirmed, on the way, delivered or cancelled) and to reset your password.
  • To keep our records and meet tax and accounting rules.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your information. We only email you about your orders and your account.

Who sees it

Only the people and services we need to run the shop:

  • Our team, and the delivery rider who brings your order (your name, phone number and address).
  • The services that help us run the website: the host that runs it, the provider that stores our database, the service that sends our emails, the service that stores our product photos, and Google if you choose to sign in with it. They handle your information only to provide their service to us.
  • The authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these services are outside Uganda, so your information may be processed or stored in other countries.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them for deliveries, returns, accounting and the law. We keep your account until you ask us to delete it.

Keeping it safe

This website uses HTTPS, passwords are stored in scrambled form and only our authorised staff can open the order records. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it (we may have to keep order records that the law requires us to keep). You can also object to how we use it. Tell us and we will reply as soon as we can. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about your information? Message us on WhatsApp or call +256 789 000 053 between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM EAT.
