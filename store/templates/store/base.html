SHIPPING & RETURNS

DELIVERY SCHEDULE

12–48 hours across Kampala and the Greater Kampala Metropolitan Area.

PRICING ZONES

  • Kampala Central — Free delivery.
  • Kampala Metropolitan (Wakiso, Mukono, Entebbe, etc.) — subsidized delivery rates, calculated per distance, with Bags & Beyond covering part of the rider fee.

PAYMENT OPTIONS

Pay on Delivery accepted via cash or direct Mobile Money (MTN MoMo / Airtel Money), paid to the delivery rider upon arrival.

RETURNS & EXCHANGES

  • 7-day notification window from time of delivery for returns or exchanges.
  • Item must be unused and in its original state.
  • Return shipping costs are shared between Bags & Beyond and the customer, handled case-by-case to reach a fair outcome.

TERMS & CONDITIONS

ORDER VERIFICATION

Orders placed with Pay on Delivery require a quick phone or WhatsApp confirmation of the exact drop-off location before a rider is dispatched.

PRODUCT AVAILABILITY

Stock is allocated on a first-confirmed basis.

INSPECTION ON DELIVERY

Customers may inspect the bag upon delivery before handing payment to the rider.
